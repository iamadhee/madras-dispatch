Prompting a language model well is a skill, and like most skills it is learned by failing in private. Promptster offers a place to do that. Three paths lead through 31 challenges, each graded on the spot, with no account and no setup.

The site lives at promptster.adheeban.com. Readers who finish all three paths are asked to write in, on the coupon printed in the Classifieds, so the editor can confirm that it is possible.
