Readers of the Technology section will have noticed that two of the projects printed here monitor the price of wristwatches. The Watch Project completes the set: a playground for designing them, with cases, dials, hands and straps that can be swapped on screen.

It is in beta at twp.adheeban.com. The editor, who declined to disclose how many watches he owns, describes the project as "research".
