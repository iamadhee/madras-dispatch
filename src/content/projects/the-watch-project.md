The editor owns more wristwatches than he will confirm, and has opinions about all of them. The Watch Project is where the opinions go: a playground for designing a watch on screen, with cases, dials, hands and straps that can be swapped until something looks right.

It is live at twp.adheeban.com. The editor, who declined to disclose how many watches he owns, describes the project as "research".
