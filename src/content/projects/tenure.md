Tenure asks 15 questions about a reader's role, company, skills and savings. It takes about three minutes. The answer is a score from 0 to 100, with the reason for every point, the source behind it, and a plan to raise it. A monthly bulletin, the Threat Radar, reports what moved in Indian IT.

The site has no server code, no database and no accounts. The answers stay in the reader's browser. The editor works in Indian IT himself, which this paper records as a conflict of interest. It is live at tenure.adheeban.com.
