In June 2023, at the height of the first language-model boom, a Python tool appeared on GitHub that promised to generate social-media content on its own. Give it a type and a description, it said, and it would write the script, voice the podcast and prepare the post.

LLM Influencer did exactly that, for podcasts at least, and picked up 39 stars from people who wanted a robot to do their posting for them. The accompanying blog post, "I built a tool that creates and posts AI content to social media automatically", explained the pipeline to a wider audience.

The repository is now quiet. Its author moved on to other experiments, and the robot influencer, like many human ones, has not posted in a while.
