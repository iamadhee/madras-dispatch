Adheeban Manoharan was a statistics student, not a computer science one, when Saama took him on as an intern in November 2020. He has described himself as coming from a non-technical background. The company appears not to have minded.

The internship ran alongside the final year of his degree at Madras Christian College. By June 2021 it was over, and by July a full-time offer had replaced it. The transition from statistics to software was complete, except for the habit of checking every number twice, which this paper is told never went away.
