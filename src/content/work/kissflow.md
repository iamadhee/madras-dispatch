Kissflow, the Chennai company whose low-code platform lets businesses build workflows without a developer in the room, has hired a developer. Adheeban Manoharan joined the firm in December 2023 as a Software Engineer. He works on the backend of the platform: the services, data and APIs that run underneath the drag-and-drop.

The move followed two and a half years at Saama, where he built data pipelines and web applications for life-sciences clients. The scale at Kissflow is different. The platform serves organisations in bulk, so a slow query is a headline, not a ticket.

In 2025 his team entered the company's FlowForge AI hackathon under the name 404 – Agent Not Found. The team took first prize. It was, in the end, found.
